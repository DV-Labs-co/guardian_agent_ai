# Guardian AI 2-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00-0:10 Problem

"Your smart home can detect problems. But who decides what to do?"

## 0:10-0:25 Activation

"Alexa, I'm leaving. Guardian, keep an eye on the house and help Mom if she needs anything."

Guardian activates household monitoring.

## 0:25-0:40 Expected Delivery

Ring detects an expected delivery. Guardian checks context and logs it without interrupting the owner.

This proves the agent is not a noisy alert bot.

## 0:40-1:00 Front Door

Ring detects an unknown visitor. Guardian checks context:

- owner away
- visitor not expected
- elderly parent home
- door unlock forbidden by policy

Guardian notifies the owner and blocks unsafe actions.

## 1:00-1:30 Wellness

User asks Guardian to check on Mom. Guardian starts a wellness workflow and waits for a response.

## 1:30-1:45 Safety Refusal

User asks Guardian to unlock the door. Guardian refuses because the visitor is unidentified and policy blocks automatic unlock.

## 1:45-2:00 Resolution

Mom responds through Alexa. Guardian resolves the incident and shows all clear on the Fire TV command center.
